Dataset: woven_sound.dat

Source: S. Sengupta (2010). "Modeling on Sound Transmission Loss
of Jute Needle-Punched nonwoven Fabrics Using Central
Composite Rotatable Experimental Design," Indian Journal of
Fibre & Textile Research, Vol35,#4,pp.293-297.

Description: 3-Factor Experiment conducted to maximize sound transmission
loss in non-woven fabrics. 20 runs.

Mass/Unit Area (g/cm^2)  -1.682=300,-1=442,0=650,1=858,1.682=1000
