Dataset: uranium1.dat

Source: M. Eisapour, A. Keshtkar, M.A. Moosavian, A. Rashidi (2013).
"Bioleaching of Uranium in Batch Stirred Tank Reactor: Process
Optimization using Box-Behnken Design," Annals of Nuclear Energy,
Vol. 54, pp. 245-250.

Description: Results of experiment in bioleaching uranium with 3
factors and 13 runs. Y=% Uranium recovery, X1=pulp density (w/v),
X2=agitation speed (rpm), X3=Aeration Rate (l/h).

Variables/Columns
Run Order       7-8
Pulp Density   15-16
Coded PD     23-24
Agitation Speed  30-32
Coded AS     39-40
Aeration Rate  46-48
Coded AR     55-56
Uranium recovery %   60-64
