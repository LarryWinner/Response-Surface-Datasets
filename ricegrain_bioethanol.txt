Dataset:  ricegrain_bioethanol.csv

Source: K. Mihajlovski, Z. Radovanovic, M. Carevic, S. Dimitrijevik-Brankovic
(2018). "Valorization of Damaged Rice Grains: Optimization of Bioethanol
Production by Waste Brewer's Yeast Usung an Amylolytic Potential from the
Paenibacillus Chitiolyticus CKS1," Fuel, Vol. 224, pp. 591-599.

Description: Response Surface (CCD) with 3 factors and 3 responses.
Factors: Malt Extract Concentration (%), Yeast Inoculum Concentraion (%),
and Fermentation Time (days)
Resonses: Ethanol Concentration (%), Yeast Cell Yield (log CFU/ml),
and Utilized Reducing Sugars (g/l)
Two clear typos Yeast Inoculum Concentration were replaced and coded analysis
matches published results.
