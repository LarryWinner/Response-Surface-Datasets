Dataset: honeycomb.dat

Source: R.R. Butukuri, V.P. Bheemreddy, K. Chandashekhara, and V.A. 
Samaranayake (2012). "Evaluation of Low-Velocity Impact Response of
Honeycomb Sandwich Structures Using Factorial-Based Design of
Experiments, Journal of Sandwich Structures and Materials,
14(3), pp. 339-361.

Description: 3^3 Factorial measuring 3 responses at 3 levels of 3 factors
in 54 runs (2 reps ate each of 27 combinations of factors).
Factor A: Angle Difference Between Successive Prepreg Layers  -1=30, 0=45, +1=60
Factor B: Number of Prepreg Layers  -1-4, 0=8, +1=12
Factor C: Number of Adhesive Layers  -1=1, 0=2, +1=3

Response X = Energy Absorbed (Joulles)
Response Y = Peak Contact Load  (kN)
Response Z = Maximum Deflection  (mm)


Variables/Columns
Sample #   7-8
Coded Factor A   15-16
Coded Factor B   23-24
Coded factor C   31-32
Energy Absorbed   34-40
Peak Contact Load  42-48
Max deflection     50-56
