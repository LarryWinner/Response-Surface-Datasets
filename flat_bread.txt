Dataset: flat_bread.dat

Source: P. Linko, C. Mattson, Y.Y. Linko, J. Antila (1984). "Production of
Flat Bread by Continuous Extrusion from High alpha-Amylase Flours,"
Journal of Cereal Science, Vol. 2, pp43-51

Description: Experiment varying 4 factors: Presence of malt,
Water Content, Mass temperature
at the dye, and Feed Rate on 5 resposes in flour production
of flat bread: moisture, horizontal expansion ratio, water absorption,
water solubility, and bulk density. 24 experimental runs

Variables/Columns
Water Content (%)  7-8
Mass Temperature (Celsius)  13-16
Feed Rate (g/min)   20-24
Malt      32  /* 1=Yes (6.3%), 0=No */
Product Moisture (%)   37-40
Horizontal Expansion ratio   43-48
Water absorption index    52-56
Water solubility index (%)  60-64
Bulk Density (g/l)  68-72
