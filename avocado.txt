Dataset: avocado.csv

Source: S. Kowarit, K. Sathapornprasath, S.N. Jansri (2024).
"Application of Hot Air Derived RSM conditions and Shading for
Solar Drying of Avocado Pulp and its Properties,"
Solar Energy, Vol. 278.  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2024.112768

Description:
Response Surface model run as Central Composite Design with 4 factors:
Hot air temperature (C):  -1=40, 0=55, +1=70
Drying Time (hours):      -1=13, 0=16.5, 1=20
Raw Material Thickness (cm):  -1=0.5, 0=0.75, +1=1.0
Wind Speed (meters/sec):      -1=0.12, 0=0.19, +1=0.26
Contains axial points at +/-2 for each factor
Response: Moisture Content (% db)

Variable Names:
run
hotAirTemp
dryTime
rawMatThck
windSpeed
moisture
