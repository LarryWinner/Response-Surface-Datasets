Dataset:  fuel_convert.dat

Source: P.A. Erickson and C. Liao (2007). "Statistical Validation
and an Empirical Model of Hydrogen Production Enhancement Found by
Utilizing Passive Flow Disturbance in the Steam-Reformation Process,"
Experimental Thermal and Fluid Science, Vol. 32, pp467-474

Description: 2^3 factorial experiment studying effects of 3 factors
on the percentage of methanol fuel converted in a steam-reformer.
Three replicated per treatment.
Factors:
Flow Rate    0=5 ml/min    1=20ml/min
Catalyst Size   0=Crushed (0.25cm)   1=Pelletized (0.47cm)
Number of bluff body packages   0=0 Packs  1=8Packs

Variables/Columns
Treatment    8
Flow Rate   16
Catalyst Size   24
Packages     32
Run order number   39-40
Percent conversion  44-48
