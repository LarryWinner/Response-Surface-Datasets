Source: N. Aslan (2007). "Application of Response Surface Methodology
and Central Composite Rotatble Design for Modeling the Influence of
Some Operating Variables of a Multi-Gravity Separator for Coal Cleaning,"

(1 to 9 by 2 degrees), Wash water (1 to 9 by 2 litres/min), feed solids
(10 to 50 by 10%) on 2 responses Ash Content (%) and Combustible Recovery (%)
in 30 run CCD.
