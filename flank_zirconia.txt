Dataset: flank_zirconia.dat

Source: N. Mandal, B. Doloi, and B. Mondal (2011). "Development of Flank Wear
Prediction Model of Zirconia Toughened Alumina (ZTA) Cutting Tool Using
Response Surface Methodology," International Journal of Refractory Metals
and Hard Materials, Vol. 29, pp. 273-280.

Description: 3 Factor Design, relating cutting speed (m/min),
feed rate (mm/rev), and Depth of Cut (mm) to flank wear (mu_m)
for a zirconia cutting tool.

Variables/Columns
Run #   7-8
Cutting speed   10-16
Feed rate  18-24
Depth of cut   26-32
Flank Wear   34-40
