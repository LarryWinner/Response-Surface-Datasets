Dataset: entrap_sod_bisph.dat

Source: Maha Nasr, et al (2010/11). "A Reliable Predictive Factorial Model for
Entrapment Optimization of a Sodium Bisphosphonate into Biodegradable Microspheres,"
Journal of Pharmacutical Sciences, 100:612-621.

Description: 2^4 factorial experiment (3 reps per factor combination),
measuring risedronate sodium (RS) entrapment efficiency percent (EE%)

Factors:
Type of Poly(lactide-co-glycolic acid)  (PLGA, -1 = 50:50, +1 = 75:25)
Internal Aqueous Phase Volume Ratio   (-1 = 1:2. +1 = 1:5)
Amount of PLGA         (-1 = 250mg, +1 = 500mg)
Amount of Drug         (-1 = 50mg,  +1 = 100mg)

Variables/Columns
PLGA Type      7-8
Internal aqueous Phase VR   15-16
Amount of PLGA   23-24
Amount of Drug   31-32
replicate #    40
RS EE%           43-48
