Dataset:  coconut.dat

Source: N.K. Rastogi, G. Rajesh, and T.R. Shamala (1998). "Optomization
of Enzymatic Degradation of Coconut Residue," Journal of the Science
of Food and Agriculture, Vol. 76, pp. 129-134.

Description: Central Composite Rotatable Design of 5 factors
on enzymatic degradation of coconut residue in 32 experimental runs.
Factors/Coding:
pH  4.0 to 9.0 by 1.25,   pH* = (pH-6.5)/1.25
Enzyme Conc (EC)  0 to 5 by 1.25    EC* = (EC-2.5)/1.25
Temperature (T) (Celsius)   25 to 65 by 10    T* = (T-45)/10
Water Ratio (WR)  0 to 2 by 0.5    WR* = (WR-1)/0.5
Time (t) (hours)  2 to 10 by 2     t* = (t-6)/2


Variables/Columns:
pH*  7-8
EC*  15-16
T*   23-24
WR*  31-32
t*   39-40
% Degradation   43-48
