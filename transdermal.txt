Dataset:  transdermal.csv

Source: H. Chaudhary, K. Kohli, S. Amin, P. Rathee, V. Kumar (2011).
"Optimization and Formulation Design of Gels of Diclfenac and Curcumin for
Transdermal Drug Delivery by Box-Behnken Statistical Design,"
Journal of Pharmaceutical Sciences, Vol. 100, #2, pp. 580-593.

Description:
3-factors, each at 3 levels, with 3 Response Variables. Box-Behnken
response surface design.
Factors:
Polymer Concentration (% w/w): -1=0.5, 0=1.0, +1=1.5
Ethanol Concentration (% w/w): -1=10, 0=15, +1=20
PG Concentration (% w/w): -1=5, 0=10, +1=15
Responses:
Flux of DDEA
Flux of CRM
Viscosity of Gel

Variable/Labels:
Run number  (runnum)
Coded Polymer Concentration  (polyconc)
Coded Ethanol Concentration  (ethnconc)
Coded PG Concentration   (pgconc)
Flux DDEA   (fluxddea)
Flux CRM   (fluxcrm)
Viscosity of Gel   (viscgel)
