Dataset:  glass_leach.dat

Source: A. Tuncuk, H. Ciftci, A. Ackil, A.Ognyanova, and F. Veglio (2010).
"Experimental Design and Process Analysis for Acidic Leaching of Metal-Rich 
Glass Wastes," Waste Management & Research, Vol.28, pp.445-454


Description: 2^4 factorials conducted on clear and green glass bottles
for percent reduction (negative values imply increase) of iron, aluminum,
and titanium. There were 3 observations at "design center"

Factors: Sample Ratio (%)  Low=10,  High=30
         Sulfuric acid concentration (M)    Low=0.5,, High=1.0
         Temperature (Celsius)    Low=60, High=120
         Time (minutes)      Low=60, High=120

Variables/Columns
Sample Ratio   5-8
Acid Concentration  10-16
Temperature    18-24
Time   26-32
Colorless glass iron removal %   34-40
Colorless glass aluminum removal %   42-48
Colorless glass titanium removal %   50-56
Green glass iron removal %   58-64 
Green glass aluminum removal %   66-72
Green glass titanium removal %   74-80
