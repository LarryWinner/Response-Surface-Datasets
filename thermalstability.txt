Dataset:  thermalstability.csv

Source: G. Golkarnarenji, M. Naebe, K. Badii, A.S. Milani, R.N. Jazar,
H. Khayyam (2018). "Support Vector Regression Modelling and Optimization
of Energy Consumption in Carbon Fiber Production Line," Computers and 
Chemical Engineering, Vol. 109, pp. 276-288.

Description: Results from an experiment involving energy consumption (MJ)  for
a thermal stabilization process with input factors: Temperature (C),
Space Velocity (m/h), and Stretch Ratio (%). Each factor at 4 levels in
a total of 30 experimental runs.
