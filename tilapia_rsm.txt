Dataset:  tilapia_rsm.dat

Source: J. Qiang, H. Yang, H. Wang, M.D. Kpundeh, P. Xu (2013).
"Interacting Effects of Water Temperature and Dietary Protein Level on
Hematological Parameters in Nile Tilapia Juveniles, Oreochromis
Nilooticus (L.) and Mortality Under Streptococcus Iniae Infection,"
Fish and Shellfish Immunology, Vol. 34, pp. 8-16

Description: Response Surface relating 3 hematalogical parameters
Red Blood cell (RBC), white blood cell (WBC), and Hemoglobin (Hb) to
temperature (range=20C-34C) and dietary protein level (range=25%-50%).
Mortality was also observed. Regressions were 2nd-order (main effects,
interaction, and quadratics), based on coded values. Mean response at
each treatment was "Y".
