Dataset:  crab_compost_shred.dat

Source: T.P. Cathcart, F.W. Wheaton, and R.B. Brinsfield (1986). "Optimizing
Variables Affecting Composting of Blue Crab Scrap," Agricultural Wastes,
Vol. 15, pp. 269-287.

Description: Regression of Y (CO2 Mass generated/Initial compost dry mass)
versus Carbon/Nitrogen Ratio, Temp (C), and moisture content (% wet weight)
for shredded crab scrap compost tests.

Variables/Columns
C/N Ratio   3-8
Temperature   10-16
Moisture Content   18-24
Y=compost rate     26-32
