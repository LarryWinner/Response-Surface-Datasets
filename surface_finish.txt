Dataset:  surface_finish.dat

Source: R. Levi (1965). "Evaluation of Machining Conditions by
Factorial Experiment," International Journal of Machine Tool Design
and Research, Vol.5,pp.217-221.

Description 2^4 factorial experiment relating 4 factors to roughness on
the finish of ground steel pieces (profilomerer readings, micr-inches AA)
measured across wheel marks. Low values are considered best.
Factors:
A=Hardness    0 = 220 Brinell   1 = 62 Rockwell C
B=Table Feed (ft/min)    0 = 32.8    1=82
C=Wheel mark pattern     0 = Cross-hatched   1 = Parallel
D=Cutting Fluid Type     0 = M, 1 = S

Variables/Columns
A       8
B       16
C       24
D       32
Roughness   34-40
