Dataset:   pulppaperbleach.csv

Source: R. Sridhar, V. Sivakumar, V. Prince Immanuel, J. Prakash Maran (2012).
"Development of Model for Treatment of Pulp and Paper Industry Bleaching
Effluent Using Response Surface Methodology," Environmental Progress &
Sustainable Energy, Vol. 31, #4, pp. 558-565.


Description: Results of a Box-Behnken design with 4 factors, each at
3 levels, and 3 response variables.
Factors:
Current density (mA/cm^2)
Initial pH
Elecrolyte Concentration (g/l)
Electrolysis time (min)
Responses: 
% Colour Removal
% Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD) Removal
% Biochemical Oxygen Demand (COD) Removal

Variables/labels
Run Number   (runnum)
Current Density  (X1)
Initial pH   (X2)
Electrolyte Concentration (X3)
Electrolysis Time   (X4)
% Colour Removal   (colour)
% COD Removal    (cod)
% BOD Removal    (bod)
Coded Current Density  (XC1)
Coded Initial pH   (XC2)
Coded Electrolyte Concentration (XC3)
Coded Electrolysis Time   (XC4)
