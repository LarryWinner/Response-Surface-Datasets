Dataset:  ayurvedic.csv

Source: S.F. Sayyad,B.P. Panda, S.R. Chauhari (2016). "Optimization of Process Parameters
of Ayurvedic Fermented Medicine Ajunarishta by Response Surface Methodology,"
Journal of Pharmaceutical Innovation, Vol. 11, pp. 102-108

Description:
Results of a Response Surface Experiment based on a Box_Behnken design.
n=17 runs with 1 response variable and 3 factors, run as a 2nd-order model.
Response: Y = Alcohol %
Predictors: X1 = Temperature (C) with levels 28, 34, 40
            X2 = pH with levels 3, 5, 7
            X3 = Inoculum volume (ml) with levels 0.5, 2.5, 5

Variable Names
runID
tempC
pH
inocVol
alc_Y
